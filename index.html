Pipedrive Overview & Expectations

Updated January 2026

What Is a CRM Tool and Why It Matters?

A Customer Relationship Management (CRM) tool is a centralized system that enables our sales organization to track, manage, and analyze all customer and prospect interactions throughout the sales process. It ensures that no lead or opportunity is lost and that every deal moves through a consistent sales funnel.

At Flow Service Partners, we use Pipedrive — a web-based CRM designed specifically for sales teams. Our current use is focused exclusively on sales pipeline management: tracking new opportunities, managing active deals, forecasting revenue, and maintaining accountability for sales activities.

Our Focus & Deal Entry Requirements

Every commercial opportunity must be entered and actively managed in Pipedrive.

Deal TypeEntry Requirement
Retrofits / Projects / Change-outsAll opportunities ≥ $5,000
Maintenance AgreementsAll opportunities (regardless of size)
ResidentialOptional, based on local management preference

Why Pipedrive Matters

For Salespeople

  • Increase close rates — identify bottlenecks and prioritize best opportunities
  • Stay organized — view all active deals and next steps at a glance
  • Save time — automated reminders replace manual spreadsheets
  • Build professionalism — accurate data reinforces credibility

For Management

  • Real-time visibility — view deal progress and forecasts across all companies
  • Accountability — standardized entry ensures accuracy
  • Performance insights — monitor conversion rates and coach reps
  • Forecasting — reliable data drives budgeting and goal-setting

Key Fields to Know

  • Organization — Customer's business name
  • Person — Your main contact at the organization
  • Deal — The specific opportunity; include org name, building, and person
  • Value — Maintenance: Year-1 value (12-month). Multiyear: average annual value
  • Proposal Date — When the proposal was provided to the customer
  • Expected Close Date — Estimated date for signed proposal or PO
  • Source Origin — Where the lead originated (marketing, Atlas, referral, existing customer)

Deal Stages

Prospect Meeting Survey / Quoting Verify Pricing Proposal Submitted Negotiations Verbal Commitment Paused Sales Won Sales Lost

Usage & Update Expectations

  • When to enter — Maintenance: when a site survey begins
  • When to enter — Projects: when pricing or scoping begins
  • How often to update: review and update all open deals weekly
  • After closing: move to Sales Won or Sales Lost with all required fields complete

Reporting & Review Cadence

FrequencyResponsibilityFocus

Key Takeaways

  • Pipedrive is the single source of truth for our sales funnel
  • Every opportunity must be entered and maintained accurately
  • Consistent use improves organization, accountability, and close rates
  • Data quality directly drives how we measure success and plan growth
