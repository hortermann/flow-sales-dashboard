Pipedrive Overview & Expectations

Updated January 2026

What Is a CRM and Why It Matters?

A CRM is a centralized system that enables our sales org to track, manage, and analyze all customer and prospect interactions. At Flow Service Partners, we use Pipedrive — focused exclusively on pipeline management: tracking opportunities, managing deals, forecasting revenue, and maintaining accountability.

Deal Entry Requirements

Deal TypeEntry Requirement
Retrofits / Projects / Change-outsAll opportunities ≥ $5,000
Maintenance AgreementsAll opportunities (regardless of size)
ResidentialOptional, based on local management preference

Why Pipedrive Matters

For Salespeople: Increase close rates, stay organized, save time with automated reminders, and build professionalism with accurate data.

For Management: Real-time visibility, accountability, performance insights, and reliable forecasting for budgeting and goals.

Key Fields to Know

  • Organization — Customer's business name
  • Person — Your main contact at the organization
  • Deal — The specific opportunity; include org name, building, and person
  • Value — Maintenance: Year-1 value. Multiyear: average annual value
  • Proposal Date — When the proposal was provided to the customer
  • Expected Close Date — Estimated date for signed proposal or PO
  • Source Origin — Where the lead originated

Deal Stages

Prospect Meeting → Survey / Quoting → Verify → Pricing → Proposal Submitted → Negotiations → Verbal Commitment → Paused → Sales Won / Sales Lost

Usage & Update Expectations

  • Maintenance deals: Enter when a site survey begins
  • Project deals: Enter when pricing or scoping begins
  • Update frequency: Review and update all open deals weekly
  • After closing: Move to Sales Won or Sales Lost with all required fields complete

Reporting & Review Cadence

FrequencyResponsibilityFocus
WeeklySalespersonUpdate pipeline and review open deals
Bi-Weekly / MonthlySales ManagerReview pipeline accuracy and forecasts
QuarterlyLeadershipEvaluate close rates, trends, and training needs
Key Takeaways: Pipedrive is the single source of truth for our sales funnel. Every opportunity must be entered and maintained accurately. Data quality directly drives how we measure success and plan growth.

Data Axle — Free Library Access Guide

How to access 16M+ business records at no cost

🆓 Why it's free Data Axle (formerly Reference USA) is licensed by thousands of public libraries across the US. Your public library card is your login — no subscription needed.

What You Can Do With Data Axle

  • Search 16M+ US businesses by industry, geography, employee count, and revenue
  • Build targeted prospecting lists of commercial building owners and facility managers
  • Find decision-maker names, phone numbers, and addresses
  • Filter by business age, ownership type, and more
  • Export lists directly to CSV

How to Access It — Step by Step

  1. 1
    Get a library card (if you don't have one) Most public libraries offer free digital e-cards you can sign up for online in minutes. Search "[your city] library card signup" or visit your city's library website.
  2. 2
    Go to your local library's website Find the research databases section — usually under "Research," "Databases," or "eResources."
  3. 3
    Look for "Data Axle" or "Reference Solutions" It may be listed as Data Axle Reference Solutions, Reference USA, or Data Axle Business.
  4. 4
    Log in with your library card number You'll be prompted to enter your library card number and sometimes a PIN — usually the last 4 digits of your phone number.
  5. 5
    Search and build your prospect list Use "U.S. Businesses" search. Filter by geography, NAICS/SIC code, employee count, and annual revenue. Export results as CSV.
